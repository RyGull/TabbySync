TabbySync

Self-hosted sync for your bookmarks and your open tabs — two tools in one browser extension, sharing one server, one token and one sync name.

No server of ours, ever. Your bookmarks and tabs go directly from your browser to a destination you choose and control: a server you self-host, a private GitHub Gist, or a JSONBin.io bin. Nothing passes through any server operated by TabbySync's developer, and there is no account, no analytics and no telemetry of any kind.

What it does

  • Bookmarks — keeps your bookmark tree in sync across your computers, merging changes rather than overwriting them.
  • Tabs — save the tabs in a window as a named list and reopen it anywhere, optionally restored as a native browser tab group.
  • Optional end-to-end encryption — AES-256-GCM with a key derived from a passphrase that never leaves your device, so the destination stores ciphertext it cannot read.
  • Use either half on its own, or both. They share one destination and one token.
