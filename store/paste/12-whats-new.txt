New install? The popup now offers a guided setup: click "Walk me through it" for a step-by-step wizard through the same four setup questions, one at a time, with Back and Next. "Set up manually" opens Settings exactly as before — both end up in the same place.

The popup and Settings pages are also tidier: the popup fits without scrolling, and Settings puts the Windows Control Panel app front and center for anyone who has it, with a real screenshot instead of one fixed picture.
