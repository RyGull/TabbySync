TabbySync now runs on Firefox, from the same source as the Chrome version.

Fixes a Firefox bug where synced bookmarks arrived in the Bookmarks Menu (hidden by default) instead of the toolbar, and one where "Save and connect" reported that access wasn't granted without ever asking for it.

The settings page no longer builds any part of itself out of text you typed, closing an injection in the server address preview.

Reopening a large list of saved tabs asks first and opens them in batches you can stop, instead of opening hundreds at once. A sync that would delete most of the bookmarks in your browser stops and asks which copy is right.
